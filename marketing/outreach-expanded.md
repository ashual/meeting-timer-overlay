## Message to carry across every channel

**Keep time. Keep the agenda moving.** Meeting Timer Overlay makes time visible in Google
Slides meetings with multiple speakers or topics and a fixed end time. A section can be an
agenda item, presentation, exercise, or discussion. One person can lead several sections.

Mark section-start slides in desktop Chrome. Slide transitions or manual section controls
move the timer forward. At a transition, the remaining meeting budget is divided among the
remaining sections. It does not enforce limits, guarantee equal airtime, listen for speakers,
or run over arbitrary apps. See [positioning.md](positioning.md).

Google Slides is a qualification question, not the definition of the audience. Start with
organizers already using a shared Slides deck. Keep long breaks or deliberately unequal
agenda blocks outside a timed block when the equal-share model is unsuitable. The interactive
demo is a click-driven simulation of the rule, not the actual extension connected to Slides.

## Opportunity 11 — Training Industry outline for an author-written proposal

Editorial route: editor@trainingindustry.com, as published in its author guidelines.
This is an internal outline, **not a send-ready article or AI-written submission**.
The publisher permits AI-assisted research/brainstorming/outlining but requires author-written
content and excludes product/service mentions. The maker should write any inquiry and article
in their own voice, verify sources independently, and disclose significant AI assistance.

- Proposed topic: Making time visible across instruction, activities, and discussion.
- Audience: trainers who have a fixed session end time and several learning activities.
- Practical question: how can a trainer signal a transition before the last activity gets rushed?
- Outline: agree the session deadline; distinguish fixed-duration activities from flexible
  discussion; make the clock visible; agree a wrap-up signal; decide explicitly what to defer;
  review which activities were rushed after the session.
- Example: a clearly labeled hypothetical session, not a claim about a training program run
  by the maker. Preserve deliberate differences in activity duration.
- Product boundary: explain the practice without naming or linking Meeting Timer Overlay in
  the article. A company link is permitted in a short author bio under the current guidelines;
  whether a project link qualifies needs editorial agreement.
- Preparation: establish the maker's actual relevant experience or collaborate with a trainer;
  do not invent L&D credentials. Independently verify sources. If invited, follow the current
  700–1,100-word guidance and Word-document format. Any rights agreement is a later review step.

This route serves the educational growth plan only if the maker wants to author an original
piece. It is lower priority than the explicit tool-suggestion channels.

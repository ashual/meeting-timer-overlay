# Free growth experiments

Targets are hypotheses: 10 organizers use it in a real meeting, and 5 return for another.
There are no observed results yet. Install counts do not establish meeting use or retention.
Do not record private contact information in this public repository. Keep an organizer-level
pilot log privately with consent and use anonymous IDs in a public aggregate report.

| Experiment | Initial batch | Qualified replies | Confirmed first meetings | Confirmed repeat meetings | Reported setup issues | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Relevant organizers already known to the maker | 10 invitations | Unknown | Unknown | Unknown | Unknown | Pending |
| LinkedIn demonstration | 1 post | Unknown | Unknown | Unknown | Unknown | Pending |
| Shareable meeting template | 5 pilot organizers | Unknown | Unknown | Unknown | Unknown | Pending |
| Technical article | 1 article | Unknown | Unknown | Unknown | Unknown | Pending |
| Creator/newsletter pitches | 5 tailored pitches | Unknown | Unknown | Unknown | Unknown | Pending |

Record the date range with each update. Leave missing observations unknown. Count first
meetings and repeat meetings separately. Ask what was hard about setup and whether the last
presenter had enough time. Request candid feedback after actual use.

Website search measurements belong in a separate record: Search Console date range,
indexed pages, relevant queries, impressions and clicks. Extension usage is not observable
from Search Console. UTM parameters alone do not provide attribution.

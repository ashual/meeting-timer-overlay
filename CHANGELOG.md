# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-24)

First public release.

- Turn timer overlay for Google Slides that splits the time left evenly between the sections
  that are left, recalculated at every handoff.
- Section list in slide order with presented, current, and upcoming states.
- Compact strip after 5 seconds without a slide change, showing the minutes, sections left,
  and progress dots.
- See-through card that stays readable on any slide background.
- Deck scanning that suggests section names from slide titles and leaves out hidden slides.
- Keyboard shortcuts to advance a section (`Alt+Shift+N`) and show or hide the overlay
  (`Alt+Shift+O`).
